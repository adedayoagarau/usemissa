#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

The expanded rail is calm but generic. The collapsed and menu states fail the product slop test because the interface appears assembled rather than governed. The deterministic CLI scan found 3 findings across 2 rules: two Inter warnings that are not product defects, and one genuine padding layout transition. Browser detection reported 23 summary findings and 25 detail messages. Genuine issues include 3.8:1 navigation labels, 2.6:1 avatar initials, and padding animation. Overflow and photographic-title findings require manual review and are mostly intentional or unreliable.

#### Overall Impression

The shell has recognizable labels and restrained color, but it loses trust at exactly the moments where it should compress or reveal context. The single largest opportunity is to define one coherent navigation and overlay grammar.

#### What's Working

- Expanded labels use plain product nouns.
- Display name and public handle are correctly separated.
- The neutral palette supports Missa's calm product register.

#### Priority Issues

1. **[P0] Collapsed navigation hides its own icon wrapper.** Hide only text, preserve persistent icons, reduce rail width, and provide tooltips.
2. **[P1] Route and discovery-filter state are conflated.** Primary navigation and category filters need separate components and emphasis.
3. **[P1] The icon and component vocabulary is mixed.** Migrate the shell to one Tabler mapping with canonical sizes.
4. **[P1] Account and notification overlays lack standard behavior.** Add Escape, outside click, focus entry and return, correct menu semantics, and 44px targets.
5. **[P2] Organization workspace IA is ambiguous and duplicated.** Make it an explicit context switcher and remove the account-menu duplicate.

#### Persona Red Flags

- A first-time writer cannot tell whether Grants + prizes navigated or filtered, then sees blank controls after collapsing.
- A keyboard or assistive-technology user encounters a menu role without menu keyboard behavior or predictable dismissal.
- An experienced creator or organization administrator gains little space from the 104px rail and cannot identify which Organization workspace will open.

#### Minor Observations

- Category icons are smaller than workspace icons.
- The full wordmark is not a compact brand asset.
- The account summary repeats the trigger identity.
- The red notification dot must disappear when there are no unread updates.
- Sidebar padding should not animate.

#### Questions to Consider

- Why should opportunity categories live in global navigation if they only modify Explore?
- What should Organization workspace open for an account with multiple memberships?
- What would the shell look like if exactly one item could receive primary active emphasis?
