How Missa works

1

Tell Missa what you make.

Choose your disciplines, working methods, location, career stage, and the kinds of support you are looking for.

2

Set what matters.

Filter out application fees, unsuitable locations, low funding, impossible deadlines, or applications heavier than you can take on.

3

See the reasoning.

Missa explains the practical reasons an opportunity may fit. No mysterious percentage. No “perfect match” claim.

4

Keep moving.

Save opportunities, compare requirements, track deadlines, and move each application from saved to submitted.

Every listing should keep its source.

Missa does not ask you to trust a summary without evidence. Each opportunity keeps the original source, important dates, verification status, and the last time its details were checked.
