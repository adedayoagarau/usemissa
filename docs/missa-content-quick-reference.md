# Missa content quick reference

> Say what people need to know. Then stop.

Missa is **plain, specific, and familiar**.

## Five rules

1. Give each message one job.
2. Put the answer, state, or action first.
3. Add another sentence only for a fact, consequence, reason, source, next action, or recovery step.
4. Do not manufacture warmth or infer feelings.
5. Stop when the task is complete.

## Before adding a sentence

It must provide something the person needs to know or do. Tone is not a reason.

**Write**

> We’ll send you an invite when Missa is ready.

**Not**

> No password yet. Just a note when Missa is ready for you.

## Limits

| Component | Default |
| :-- | :-- |
| Headline | 2–8 words; one promise or task |
| Supporting line | 1 sentence; up to 24 words |
| Button | 1–3 words; verb + object when possible |
| Helper text | 1 sentence; up to 18 words |
| Toast | Up to 8 words; completed action |
| Empty state | 1–2 sentences; absence + action |
| Error | 1–3 sentences; failure + safe state + recovery |

## Reject

- **No X. Just Y.**
- **Your X is Y. Your Z should be too.**
- **Less X. More Y.**
- **From X to Y.**
- **Whether you’re…**
- **At Missa, we believe…**
- journey, possibility, and “work ahead” language;
- three abstract benefits written for rhythm;
- endings about focusing on “what matters most.”

Remove: **calm, meaningful, thoughtful, seamless, frictionless, intuitive, powerful, effortless, exciting, inspiring, journey, ecosystem, unlock, elevate, streamline, navigate, leverage, just, simply, ready for you, designed to help, all in one place**.

Remove **successfully** when the verb already shows success.

## Canonical homepage copy

> # Find the call worth your time.
>
> Missa helps you compare the facts, open the official source, save your decision, and keep track of what comes next.
>
> **Browse Opportunities**
>
> **How Missa handles evidence**

Do not add an eyebrow unless it gives information.

For waitlist or invitation surfaces, use the same value in a shorter form:

> Find the calls that fit your work, prepare what you need, and stay on top of every deadline—with the original source close at hand.

## Product patterns

**Buttons:** Browse opportunities · Build your profile · Save opportunity · Add work · Track submission · View decision

**Helper text:** Explain why, format, visibility, or consequence.

> PDF, DOCX, or TXT. Maximum 25 MB.

**Empty state:** Name what is absent, then give one action.

> **No saved opportunities**
>
> Save an opportunity to find it here.

**Success:** Name the completed action.

> Profile saved. · Opportunity saved. · Submission sent.

**Error:** State what failed, what remains safe, and how to recover.

> We could not save your profile. Your previous version is still available. Try again.

## Taxonomy language

Use **What do you make?** or the specific value a person selected. Do not use **practice**, **practice family**, **creative practice**, **creative field**, or **field** as a label for the kind of work someone makes. Do not expose **facet**, **taxonomy**, **term ID**, or **matching input** in product copy.

**Field** is still allowed for ordinary form language: **email field**, **required field**, and **field error**.

Public Profile headings use **About**, **Selected Works**, **Works**, **Publications**, and **Links**. Private controls use **Preferences**, **Notifications**, **Integrations**, **Privacy**, **Security**, and **Account**.

## Matching and evidence

Use observable fit reasons:

> **Why this fits**
>
> Installation art · Open to West Africa · No application fee

Do not infer quality, identity, financial need, career potential, or likelihood of acceptance.

Keep unknown information exact: **Fee not stated · Fee not confirmed · Deadline not confirmed · Location requirement not stated · Required materials not confirmed**.

Keep the official source close: **Open official source · Read official guidelines**.

Freshness scores, check times, and refresh state are backend operations—not customer-facing opportunity copy.

## Difficult moments

Use the actual state: **Submitted · Received · In review · Shortlisted · Accepted · Declined · Waitlisted · Withdrawn**.

> The organization declined this submission. The decision message and submitted files remain available in Missa.

No consolation, jokes, or motivational follow-up.

## AI

Do not lead marketing with AI. Explain it when Missa ranks, summarizes, drafts, changes, or acts on information the person needs to understand.

> This recommendation uses your selections, location, and fee preferences. Update your Profile or hide this Opportunity.

Use **matched, suggested, summarized, extracted, grouped, drafted, flagged, checked**.

Avoid **knows, understands you, chose for you, judged, guaranteed, eliminated bias, perfect match**.

## Final check

- Is the main point first?
- Does the message do one job?
- Does every extra sentence add information?
- Are facts, sources, unknowns, and statuses exact?
- Is the action clear when needed?
- Is the wording ordinary enough to say aloud?
- Have decorative warmth and repeated meaning been removed?
- Is the copy within the component limit?
- Is high-stakes copy direct and free of jokes?
