# Writing room roadmap

The goal: a writing tool better than Scrivener, untouched by AI. How the room
works today is in `docs/writing-room.md`. This file tracks what is built and
what comes next, in the order agreed with the owner.

## Built

| Stage | What it gives the writer | Migration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Write mode | A blank page, a timer, ten open-licence typefaces, device-first saving that never overwrites another device, entries, download | 0095 |
| Pages | Rich text on printed pages (A4, US Letter, A5, chapbook), a format for each page, every space and tab kept, titles, print and save as PDF | 0096 |
| Projects | Projects from templates, a binder to order pieces, loose pieces, an outline of synopses and statuses, compile to one manuscript | 0097 |

## Next, in order

1. **Free canvas.** Any page can switch from flowing text to a canvas where
   blocks of text sit where the writer puts them, for concrete and
   experimental poetry. Printed exactly as placed.
2. **Snapshots, find and replace, dark mode.** A snapshot of a piece at any
   time, compared side by side with the current text and restorable; find and
   replace across a piece's pages; a dark writing room.
3. **Writing for a call.** Tie a piece or project to a call: its prompts and
   limits beside the draft, a live count against its word or page limit, and a
   check that your name isn't in material read blind, reusing the pre-submit
   check.

## Later

- Export to Word and standard manuscript format.
- Word targets for a session or a project, shown calmly, with no streaks.
- Research beside the draft: notes, images and PDFs in a split view.
- Corkboard view of the outline's index cards.
- Import from Word, Scrivener (`.scriv`) and Google Docs.
- "Make this a Work" and "Send to Tracker", so each version is tied to the
  call it went to.
- Feedback from a reading partner, from people only.

## Open decisions

- Default typeface: Newsreader now; EB Garamond draws every Yoruba, Igbo and
  Hausa letter checked.
- The one use of "AI" on the page, in the privacy promise, needs an owner
  decision before public launch.
- The cookie banner prints with the pages because it is global.
