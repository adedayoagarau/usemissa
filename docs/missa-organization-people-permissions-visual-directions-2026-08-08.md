# Missa Organization People and permissions visual directions

Date: 8 August 2026  
Status: Option 2 selected in the local library and implemented as a read-only local Organization route; mutations and production promotion blocked  
Selected review route: `/design-system/organization-people-permissions`  
Comparison route: `/design-system/organization-people-permissions-directions`

## 01 — Directory ledger

A compact table-led administration view. It is strongest for large directories, invitations, and bulk review, but role and scope consequences can become too terse.

## 02 — Access dossier — selected

A master-detail view that keeps the directory and one person's effective access together. Role bundle, resource scope, assignments, seat class, provisioning source, and removal safeguards are visible before an administrator acts. Mobile becomes a directory-first, full-page person detail with Back.

Selected because it gives the safest cross-role view of effective access and mutation consequences, preserves fast directory scanning, and has the clearest mobile transformation. The directory ledger remains available for large portfolios and the Team map remains a future scoped-membership view.

## 03 — Team map

A grouped view by Entity/team and Program. It makes scope legible but is aspirational until scoped memberships exist as first-class data.

## Shared visual rules

- true white canvas with Aubergine emphasis;
- Lichen for active/complete, Ochre for attention, Mineral Blue for neutral information;
- no presence dots, gamified seat gauges, organization charts that imply employment hierarchy, or gradient admin cards;
- commercial seat, role bundle, scope, invitation state, and account state remain separate labels;
- every mutation has a review step and durable success/failure feedback;
- all three directions remain available in the local comparison switcher;
- product promotion remains blocked.
