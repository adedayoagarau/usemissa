# Homepage redesign: Mobbin references

6 October 2026. Reference research for the homepage redesign that follows the
critique recorded in `.impeccable/critique/2026-10-06T00-34-32Z__apps-web-app-page-tsx.md`.

Direction agreed: structure first, quiet editorial middle, full scope, no section
off-limits. These references are pattern evidence, not a theme to copy. Missa
tokens, type and components stay as DESIGN.md defines them.

Mobbin has no direct arts-opportunity platform. The closest domain matches are a
creative jobs board (Craft Agency) and an events app (Luma). Everything else is
borrowed for one structural idea at a time.

## Anti-references seen in the same searches

- [Eventbrite hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/3c1c20a0-c8d6-4030-a0f9-79e2d5ede604): heavy sans over a busy photograph, the thing the current hero avoids.
- [Zendesk upcoming events](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/335b7f42-9dc5-4e79-bdea-6796a4ef30c7): a card carousel with arrows for six items, the pattern we are removing.
- [Apollo community grid](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5e409534-a2ac-4bf2-afc8-e85b69a1eabb) and [Visual Electric hero](https://mobbin.com/screens/7227285b-1e87-4628-b822-ca1adca474e3): photo collages as decoration.
- [Pi onboarding](https://mobbin.com/screens/bed06e42-b8e2-4d77-9e1a-adcca3fa0b18): illustrated cream canvas, the PRODUCT.md anti-reference.

## What this changes in the plan

1. The hero absorbs the three live totals as a fact strip; the citron slab goes.
2. "Open now" is a serif statement, one featured call, five deadline-led rows, then the category row with counts. No carousel.
3. The explorer becomes a two-item list beside one fixed-height framed excerpt.
4. Organisations is a named two-column list with small photographs.
5. The footer ends on the wordmark, not a painting.
