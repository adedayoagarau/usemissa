# Education and fellowships source map

## Recommendation

Missa should expand into education through a bounded **fellowships and funded-study pilot**, not a single catch-all education category. The first source tranche should combine:

1. high-volume official indexes that expose many current programmes;
2. official programme families with strong structured eligibility and funding details; and
3. a small set of discovery-only aggregators used to find independent programmes such as IAPS.

The initial pilot should monitor **IAPS, UNESCO, TWAS, EURAXESS, DAAD, Erasmus Mundus, MSCA, Chevening, Commonwealth Scholarships, Fulbright Foreign Student, and EducationUSA**. ProFellow and Opportunity Desk should enter only as discovery desks whose outbound first-party destinations must be resolved and verified.

This is a source-research artifact, not approval to add registry entries, run crawlers, write canonical records, or publish opportunities.

## Why IAPS is a useful seed

The IAPS Policy Fellowship is educational, professional, and experiential at once: it is a fully funded three-month programme with research and applied tracks, a two-week in-person opening in Washington, D.C., followed by ten weeks of mentored work. The official page also distinguishes D.C., London, and remote participation, and points to a separate GuidedTrack application.^1

That makes it a good test of the fields a creator-only model can easily miss:

- programme mode: in-person, remote, or hybrid;
- track or pathway;
- cohort and programme dates, distinct from the application deadline;
- education/career stage rather than artistic discipline alone;
- citizenship, residence, work-location, degree, and experience requirements;
- stipend, tuition, travel, visa, accommodation, and other support as separate benefits;
- host, mentor, partner, and application-platform roles;
- degree-bearing versus non-degree-bearing status;
- expected outputs, such as research, training, placement, or policy work.

IAPS itself is an **official publisher source**. Its programme page can establish the programme description and recurring cohort information; GuidedTrack can establish application state. Neither should silently inherit the other’s claims.

## Source tiers

### Priority 1: official multi-programme sources

| Source | Coverage | Why it is valuable | Source role | Pilot priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [UNESCO Fellowships](https://www.unesco.org/en/fellowships) | Study, PhD, early-career research, and project fellowships | One official hub links to UNESCO, TWAS, OWSD, and co-sponsored programmes; it reports more than 450 fellowships across its portfolio.^2 | Official programme-family index; some children lead to partner authorities | A |
| [TWAS fellowships](https://twas.org/opportunities/fellowships/phd) | PhD, sandwich, postdoctoral, and visiting-research programmes, especially for researchers from developing countries | Detail-rich pages expose call state, host country, eligible nationality, minimum degree, duration, discipline, and age limit.^3 | Official programme-family index and publisher | A |
| [EURAXESS](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs/search) | Research jobs, PhDs, postdocs, research fellowships, grants, and hosting offers | High-volume, current index with filters for offer type, country, research field, researcher profile, academic level, funding programme, and funding type.^4 | Public institutional discovery/hosting platform; verify the hiring or funding institution where needed | A |
| [DAAD Scholarship Database](https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/) | Study, research, language, doctoral, postdoctoral, teacher, and artist funding in Germany | Strong applicant-oriented filters for country of origin, academic status, purpose, and subject; includes DAAD programmes and selected external funders.^5 | DAAD is authority for DAAD awards; external awards require publisher verification | A |
| [Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters) | International joint master’s degrees and scholarships | Official catalogue route for degree-bearing, multi-country programmes; applications go to each consortium, whose site contains current requirements and application steps.^6 | Official programme-family index; consortium is application authority | A |
| [MSCA funding](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/mt/funding) | Doctoral networks, postdoctoral fellowships, staff exchanges, and co-funded programmes | Official calls expose stable call references, open/forthcoming/closed status, dates, and links to the EU Funding & Tenders portal.^7 | Structured authority plus application portal | A |
| [Chevening award finder](https://www.chevening.org/apply/) | UK master’s scholarships and named professional fellowships | Official country/territory and award finder with current application state; country rules must remain explicit rather than generalized globally.^8 | Official programme-family index and publisher | A |
| [Commonwealth Scholarships](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/) | Master’s, PhD, professional, startup, and host-linked fellowships | Official programme archive with distinct candidate, host, eligibility, finance, and application guidance.^9 | Official programme-family index and publisher | A |
| [Fulbright Foreign Student Program](https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/about/foreign-student-program) | Graduate study and research in the US for non-US applicants | Operates in more than 160 countries, but eligibility, process, and deadlines are administered by country-specific commissions or US embassies.^10 | Global index; country commission/embassy is operational authority | A |
| [EducationUSA financial aid](https://educationusa.state.gov/find-financial-aid) | Scholarships and special opportunities at US institutions for international students | Searchable official US-government network listing with degree level, state/location, institution, and deadlines; currently mixes annual and dated entries, so freshness requires destination checks.^11 | Government discovery index; institution is award authority | B |

### Priority 2: official programme families to add after the pilot

These sources are high quality but narrower. They should be onboarded as separate source contracts after the initial field model is proven:

- OWSD fellowships for women scientists from eligible developing countries;
- national research councils and academies, such as UKRI, DFG, JSPS, Swiss Government Excellence Scholarships, and Australia Awards;
- university graduate-funding and fellowship offices;
- major named scholarship families such as Rhodes, Gates Cambridge, Schwarzman, Knight-Hennessy, and Mastercard Foundation Scholars;
- policy, public-interest, journalism, technology, climate, health, and leadership fellowship hosts similar to IAPS;
- official multilateral organizations such as the UN, World Bank, IMF, OECD, and regional development banks where they publish fellowships, scholarships, or young-professional programmes.

Each is authoritative only for its own programme family. A university list of outside fellowships is still a discovery source for those outside awards.

### Priority 3: discovery-only sources

| Source | Strength | Limitation | Treatment in Missa |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ProFellow](https://www.profellow.com/) | Large curated database of 2,800+ fellowships, awards, and funded graduate programmes; strong professional-fellowship coverage.^12 | US-oriented; account access and commercial listings may constrain systematic use; it is not the award publisher | Candidate discovery only; store outbound official URL and discard copied deadlines once the official page is resolved |
| [Opportunity Desk](https://opportunitydesk.org/about/) | Broad global youth coverage across fellowships, scholarships, training, conferences, grants, internships, and volunteering.^13 | Editorial aggregator; individual posts can age or duplicate programme pages | Candidate discovery only; no publication authority |
| University fellowship lists | Often reveal niche and international-eligible programmes missed by general search | Lists are commonly static PDFs or advising resources and may lag current cycles | Discovery leads; verify every programme at the funder/host |
| Newsletters, social feeds, and community submissions | Excellent early signal for small or new programmes | Weakest identity, date, and terms guarantees | Community signal; quarantine until a first-party destination is proven |

## The first operating tranche

Start with eleven source contracts, in this order:

1. **IAPS** — one bounded official programme and one application platform; validates the new fellowship fields.
2. **TWAS** — unusually structured fellowship detail pages and explicit open/closed state.
3. **UNESCO** — a navigable parent hub that tests partner-child authority.
4. **EURAXESS** — high-volume research discovery with rich facets and institutional children.
5. **DAAD** — applicant-origin and education-stage eligibility.
6. **Erasmus Mundus** — degree programme plus consortium plus scholarship relationships.
7. **MSCA** — stable call identifiers, host relationships, and EU portal handoff.
8. **Chevening** — country-specific award variants and a shared annual cycle.
9. **Commonwealth Scholarship Commission** — multiple scholarship/fellowship types and nominated application paths.
10. **Fulbright Foreign Student** — a global brand whose actual authority is delegated country by country.
11. **EducationUSA** — institution-level financial-aid discovery and freshness testing.

Only after those contracts pass should ProFellow and Opportunity Desk be used to widen the long tail.

## Required model changes before scale

Do not make `educational` an opportunity type. It is better represented as a product domain or collection spanning independent dimensions.

Minimum opportunity types:

- fellowship;
- scholarship;
- funded degree;
- research position;
- exchange;
- training programme;
- summer or winter school;
- professional development programme.

Minimum independent fields:

- education level: secondary, undergraduate, master’s, doctoral, postdoctoral, professional, or unrestricted;
- career stage and years of experience;
- degree-bearing status and credential;
- study/research field;
- citizenship and residence eligibility;
- host country, required presence, remote availability, and mobility rule;
- programme start/end, cohort label, application open/deadline, and recurring-cycle confidence;
- tuition coverage, stipend, travel, visa, insurance, accommodation, dependants, and other support;
- nomination, host-matching, admission-first, and parallel-application requirements;
- return-home, service, employment, or publication obligations;
- application destination and whether the applicant, host institution, or nominating body submits.

This preserves Missa’s existing separation of opportunity type, eligibility, geography, taxonomy, source role, and authority.

## Source acceptance test

Before a source becomes runnable, record:

- publisher/owner and public organization address;
- source role and authority boundary;
- index URL, child URL pattern, canonical programme URL, and application URL;
- access method: API, RSS, sitemap, bounded index, detail page, newsletter, or manual-only;
- stable programme/call identifier;
- pagination and archive behavior;
- open, forthcoming, closed, paused, and recurring states;
- last-updated evidence and expected refresh cadence;
- robots, terms, rate limits, authentication, and redistribution restrictions;
- one open, one closed, one country-specific, and one missing-field fixture where available.

Publication should still require an official destination, reconciled organizer identity, current application state, field evidence, and the existing publication gate. A directory or programme index can discover a record without becoming authority for all of its claims.

## Pilot success criteria

The tranche is ready to scale when it can:

- extract and reconcile at least 50 varied opportunities across the eleven sources;
- preserve country- and applicant-specific eligibility without flattening it into `global`;
- distinguish scholarship, fellowship, degree, job, grant, and training records consistently;
- represent multi-party authority correctly for Erasmus, Fulbright, MSCA, and UNESCO/TWAS;
- detect deadline and cohort rollover without cloning the same programme blindly;
- keep closed or undated records out of public “open now” results;
- explain every public field with first-party evidence;
- pass a manual review sample before any bulk publication.
