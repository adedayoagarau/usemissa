# Missa homepage: Submittable-inspired direction

The local review route now follows the structural rhythm of Submittable’s
current homepage: announcement strip, product navigation, large promise,
product proof, audience/solution sections, workflow, and a final action.

Missa does not copy Submittable’s brand, wording, imagery, logos, testimonials,
metrics, or product claims. The translation uses Missa’s own promise,
repository-backed Opportunity data, evidence language, and access boundary.

## Translation

- Submittable’s program-management hero becomes Missa’s “Find the call worth
  your time.”
- Product screenshots become a CSS-built Missa catalogue shell.
- Program audiences become creators and organizations.
- Grant-program workflow becomes Read → Decide → Track.
- Testimonials and customer metrics become source, deadline, fee, and unknown
  states.
- Demo/sales CTAs become Browse Opportunities and How Missa handles evidence.

The page uses CSS-only atmospheric color and UI composition. No generated image
or external stock image is required for the primary surface.

## Boundary

This remains `/design-system/homepage-future`, a local review concept. It does
not replace the production root, proxy, public repository, authentication,
waitlist, or Save-to-Tracker contracts.
