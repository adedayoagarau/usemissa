# Creator-facing competitors: open-call discovery and submission tracking (writers, visual artists, film)

Research date: 2026-10-09. Every figure carries the date of its source where one was available. "Retrieved 2026-10" means the figure was read from a live page during this research and the page itself was undated. Third-party and estimated data are flagged with **[3rd-party]** or **[estimate]**.

Context for the reader: Missa lists open calls for artists and writers, with fees, eligibility, deadline reminders and a submission tracker. It has a free tier and Plus at $6/month or $60/year.

---

## 1. Competitor profiles: who they are, what they cost, what they cover

### Takeaway
For writers, the market comes down to one paid incumbent (Duotrope, which since June 2026 charges $6/month or $60/year, exactly Missa Plus pricing), one fast-growing freemium challenger (Chill Subs, with about 84k users in April 2026), one donation-funded free tool (Submission Grinder), and many free newsletters. Visual-art platforms (CaFE, Zealous, EntryThingy, ArtCall, Artwork Archive, FilmFreeway for film) are mostly free for creators, because the organisers who run the calls pay. That leaves room for a cross-discipline, creator-paid discovery and tracking layer. No incumbent covers writers and visual artists equally well.

### Inferences
- Duotrope's move to $6/month or $60/year in June 2026 means **Missa Plus is now priced exactly at the category incumbent**. Missa cannot compete as the cheaper option on headline price. It has to compete on a free tier (Duotrope has none), on cross-discipline coverage, and on fee and eligibility transparency.
- Chill Subs Pro, at $10/month or $96/year, sets a higher price ceiling for a modern writer UX. The market will bear $6–$10 a month from writers.
- On the visual-art side, creators are used to paying nothing to the platform, because organisers pay (CaFE, Zealous, EntryThingy, FilmFreeway). The exceptions are ArtDeadline (about $9–$24 a year) and Artwork Archive inventory plans. A creator-paid Plus tier will be harder to sell to visual artists than to writers.
- No player covers writers and visual artists with equal depth. Duotrope comes closest (1,497 visual-art markets, but writer-first). CaFE and Zealous list only calls they host.

## 3. Growth, decline, acquisitions, complaints and recent news (2024–2026)

### Takeaway
The writer market is shifting from Duotrope, which is paid, closed and raised its price in 2026, toward Chill Subs, which is freemium, community-led and adding features quickly. The 2013 Duotrope paywall backlash is the founding myth of the free alternatives. Consolidation has come through enterprise moves (Backstage buying FilmFreeway in 2021; Submittable buying WizeHive in 2024) and media mergers (Write or Die into Chill Subs in 2023), not creator-side tools.

### Cited Findings
- **2013 Duotrope paywall:** $5/month or $50/year from January 2013. Critics said $25–30 would be fair. The Found Poetry Review petitioned for magazines to pay instead of writers, warning that "many will cease using the site." The backlash spawned the Submission Grinder and a crowdfunding attempt, Submitomancy. — [Poetry Foundation](https://poetryfoundation.org/poetry-news/66670/shouldering-the-duotrope-fees); [Missouri Review](https://missourireview.com/tag/duotrope/); [Brent Knowles](https://blog.brentknowles.com/2013/01/13/duotrope-marketlist-replacement/). A widely cited "violent protests" article by John Fox is satire and should not be used as evidence. — [thejohnfox.com](https://thejohnfox.com/2012/12/duotrope-paid-subscriptions/)
- **Duotrope price rise, June 2026:** the first change since 2013, to $6/month or $60/year. — [Duotrope announcement](https://duotrope.com/news/announcement.aspx)
- **Chill Subs 2024–2026:**
  - Membership prices roughly doubled: Premium went from $5/month (2024) to Pro at $10/month (2026), and a $90/month coached tier was added.
  - New features in 2026: Red Flags, AI and reprint policy fields, an MFA database, and a rebrand.
  - It runs the Lit Mag Incubator.
  - Sources: [PW](https://www.publishersweekly.com/pw/by-topic/industrynews/publisher-news/article/94166-chill-subs-wants-to-take-the-angst-out-of-submissions.html); [Chill Subs memberships](https://support.chillsubs.com/memberships); [Apr 2026 roundup](https://www.chillsubs.com/blog/april-2026-roundup)
- **FilmFreeway:** after the 2021 Backstage acquisition, user sentiment has been "between cautious optimism and outright skepticism", with fears of commercialisation. Scam festivals remain a long-standing complaint. Gold rose from $10.99/month (2022) to $14.99/month. — [Film Daily](https://filmdaily.co/indie-film/does-film-freeway-suck-since-it-got-acquired-by-backstage/); [Film Festival Fever](https://filmfestivalfever.substack.com/p/how-to-spend-10000-on-film-festival); [FilmFreeway Gold](https://filmfreeway.com/gold)
- **Submittable:** acquired WizeHive in 2024 (adding Bright Funds and WeHero) and is moving toward grantmaking and CSR software. — [yespress.io](https://yespress.io/submittable)
- **EntryThingy:** retired its "Classic" version in March 2026, and is publishing marketing aimed at CaFE ("5 best CaFE alternatives 2026"). — [EntryThingy](https://www.entrythingy.com/pricing.html); [EntryThingy blog](https://entrythingy.com/blog/5-best-call-for-entry-cafe-alternatives-2026)
- **CaFE:** WESTAF rebranded as Creative West. CaFE introduced annual subscription pricing for public-art agencies. — [CaFE packet](https://media.callforentry.org/2025/03/26141905/CaFE-Info-Packet-2025.pdf); [Creative West](https://wearecreativewest.org/de/wp-json/wp/v2/posts/4539)
- **Signs of decline or dormancy:** Trish Hopkinson went on hiatus (2022). Winning Writers dropped its paid tier. TransArtists admits its data is stale. Authors Publish shows no subscriber update since 2021. — [Lit Mag News](https://litmagnews.substack.com/p/get-outta-my-dreams-get-into-my-lit); [WOW](https://muffin.wow-womenonwriting.com/2013/02/check-out-winning-writers-contests-and.html); [TransArtists FAQ](https://www.transartists.org/about/faq)
- **AI-generated submissions:** magazines are now commonly banning AI-assisted work (for example matchbook and Clarkesworld). Chill Subs now shows each magazine's AI policy. — [Duotrope matchbook listing](https://duotrope.com/duosuma/submit/matchbook-hi9Xx); [Chill Subs Apr 2026](https://www.chillsubs.com/blog/april-2026-roundup)

### Inferences
- Writers have shown a long-standing willingness to switch over price (2013). Duotrope's 2026 increase may push price-sensitive users to look again, which helps a free tier with tracking.
- Price inflation across the category (Chill Subs doubling, FilmFreeway Gold up 36%, Duotrope up 20%) suggests incumbents are monetising existing users rather than growing the market. That leaves room for a lower-priced or freemium cross-discipline entrant.
- Trust signals (red flags, scam-festival auditing, fee transparency) are a theme across writer and film platforms in 2024–2026. Missa's fee and eligibility fields fit this demand.

### Gaps
- No 2024–2026 user-sentiment data (Reddit threads, for example) was retrieved directly. Sentiment here comes from blogs and press.
- No press coverage of Duotrope's June 2026 price rise, or of any reaction to it, was found.

## 4. New entrants (2024–2026), including AI-powered opportunity finders

### Takeaway
No well-funded, widely adopted AI open-call finder for writers or artists turned up in 2024–2026. The AI-flavoured activity is small: Submitit's algorithmic lit-mag matching (2020), Chill Subs' human-curated Custom Lists and Lit Mag Matching, an EU STARTS AI-matching tool for art-tech, and unlaunched hackathon prototypes. The "AI opportunity matcher for creatives" space looks open but unproven.

### Cited Findings
- **Submitit** (founded 2020 by Erik Harper Klass) uses "a unique algorithm to match clients' stories and essays with the ideal literary magazines". Its current status and pricing are unverified. — [Lit Mag News interview, 2023](https://litmagnews.substack.com/p/you-have-to-read-the-journals-a-chat)
- **Chill Subs** offers paid matching: Custom Lists at $50–$150, and "Lit Mag Matching" discounted 25% for Pro members. The Inner Circle tier includes monthly matched lists. — [Custom Lists](https://subclub.substack.com/p/custom-lists-handpicked-lit-mag-submission-opportunities); [memberships](https://support.chillsubs.com/memberships)
- **STARTS (EU)** offers "AI-powered matching" to tailored opportunities for art and technology prototypes. Launch date unconfirmed. — [STARTS open calls](https://starts.eu/calls/)
- **ARTWORKS**, a Singapore hackathon prototype (2025/2026), is an "AI-powered arts exclusive platform that intelligently matches artists with opportunities". It had not been tested with real users. — [hack.gov.sg 2025](https://www.hack.gov.sg/2025/artworks)
- **Adjacent AI matchers that are not open-call tools:**
  - Persius (artist-to-buyer matching). — [Gust](https://gust.com/companies/persius)
  - UpStage (talent marketplace for actors and creators). — [UpStage](https://nitter-canary.kareem.one/UpStagePlatform)
  - OpenMic (music collaborator matching, Product Hunt). — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/openmic)
- A blog post tested using general AI (LLMs) to pick lit mags, which suggests writers already use ChatGPT-style tools ad hoc for this. — [After Dinner Conversation](https://www.afterdinnerconversation.com/news/how-to-find-the-right-literary-magazine-to-submit-your-writing-to-according-to-ai)

### Inferences
- The main AI threat is probably general-purpose assistants (ChatGPT and others) answering "where should I submit?", not a dedicated startup. Structured, verified data (fees, eligibility, deadlines) is what those assistants lack.
- Chill Subs charging $50–$150 for curated lists shows people will pay for matching. An automated equivalent inside Missa Plus could be a differentiator.

### Gaps
- Searches did not surface a funded 2024–2026 startup positioned as an "AI open-call finder" for artists or writers. Small or recent launches (Product Hunt, App Store) may have been missed. A targeted scan of Product Hunt and App Store keywords ("grant finder artists", "residency finder", "submission tracker") is recommended.
- Submitit's 2026 status and pricing were not verified.
