# Brainstorming Session Results

**Facilitator:** Adedayo
**Date:** September 8, 2026

## Session Overview

**Topic:** Invent category-defining futures for Missa beyond the conventional opportunity-platform model.

**Goals:** Generate more than 100 genuinely original concepts across technology, behavior, economics, culture, networks, and black-swan scenarios. Include both ambitious moonshots and credible ideas that could ship within 6–12 months. Delay ranking until the obvious territory has been exhausted, then identify new product primitives and defensible directions.

### Context Guidance

Missa's current repository is background context, not the boundary of imagination. Competitor feature patterns are explicitly excluded as the starting point. Existing capabilities may later make an invention feasible, but they do not count as the invention.

### Session Setup

The session may redefine what Missa is. It will deliberately alternate creative domains to resist incremental clustering and will retain unusual ideas long enough to discover stronger combinations.

## Technique Selection

**Approach:** Progressive Technique Flow

**Progressive Techniques:**

- **Phase 1 - Break the category:** Assumption Reversal and Parallel Universe Cafe for 50-60 category-breaking provocations.
- **Phase 2 - Cross-pollinate:** Forced Relationships for 40-50 collisions with unrelated systems and behaviors.
- **Phase 3 - Find what is emerging:** Emergent Thinking and Concept Blending to develop 8-12 coherent product worlds.
- **Phase 4 - Reverse-engineer reality:** Dream Fusion Laboratory to derive 6-12 month wedges, network effects, risks, and first experiments.

**Journey Rationale:** The flow protects originality by postponing feasibility and organization until more than 100 raw ideas exist. It then develops combinations rather than selecting the most familiar standalone features.
