# Story 13.1: Private progress Props

Status: review

Missa derives a small, private progress projection from a submitter's own
Tracker history: starting a shortlist, building range, making time to prepare,
and sending a first submission. Props are descriptive rather than competitive,
never mention rejection as failure, and are not exposed through public Profile,
organization Workspace, exports, or matching. The Profile page renders the
projection under “A little momentum.”
